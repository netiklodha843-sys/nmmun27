About

More than a conference.

Narsee Monjee Model United Nations puts students in the seats of ambassadors. For two days, delegates take a country's position on a real international crisis, argue it in formal session, build alliances in the corridors and turn disagreement into a written resolution.

The 18th edition at NMIMS Shirpur is built around rigorous background guides, well-trained chairs and committees that reward preparation. Delegates leave with sharper public speaking, negotiation and leadership skills, and a network that reaches beyond their own campus.
